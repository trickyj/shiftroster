Roster Notes

✦ Rotation Logic

Weekend duty rotates every week between the two people per shift pair: P1↔P2 (S1), M1↔M2 (S3), M3↔M4 (S4). The person on weekend duty takes Mon + Tue off that same week — giving them 2 consecutive comp days.

⚡ S2 & S5 — Weekdays Only

P3, P4 (S2 India) and PA1 (S5 Panama) never work weekends. They always get Sat + Sun off. This is by design — S2 and S5 are not active on weekends.

⚠ Weekend Gap: Midnight–00:30 UTC

On weekends, S4 ends at 22:00 UTC and S1 starts at 00:30 UTC. There's a ~2.5h window with no coverage. Consider an on-call arrangement or extending S4 to cover this if 24/7 weekend coverage is strictly needed.

⚠ Panama Single Point of Failure

PA1 is the only person covering S5 (17:00–01:00 UTC). On weekdays this works fine, but any leave or absence creates a gap. Consider cross-training M4 as backup for S5 when PA1 is absent.
